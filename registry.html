We have enough stuff. What we don't have nearly enough of is art made by people we love.

A drawing, a painting, a photograph, a poem written on a napkin. Any of it will mean more to us than anything from a store. Our home should feel like it was made by the people in our lives, and you're one of them.

If you're not sure where to start, we made a list of ideas below, from beginner-friendly to go-all-out.

🎨

Ideas & inspiration

Roughly ordered from easiest to most ambitious. Honestly though, any effort counts.
